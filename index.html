Our Mission

Build. Learn. Inspire.

Three pillars guide everything we build, teach, and give back — season after season.

Build

We design, machine, and program a competitive FTC robot from scratch every season — learning CAD, code, and craftsmanship along the way.

Learn

As a young team, we're growing fast — picking up new skills in engineering, business, and teamwork with every meeting.

Inspire

We're building a presence in our Plainsboro community, introducing younger students to STEM and robotics.

Season Two

Full send, season two.

We wrapped our rookie year in 2025 — now we're back, sharper, and building toward what's next.

Compete

Take the field for our second FTC season with a stronger, more capable robot than our rookie year.

Grow

Expand our roster beyond 12, and deepen every member's hands-on engineering and business skills.

Give Back

Start bringing hands-on robotics demos to younger students right here in Plainsboro.
