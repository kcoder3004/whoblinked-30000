We're a young team building our program — and our sponsor list — from the ground up. Every partner who backs us this early becomes part of our origin story.

Become a Sponsor Today

Sponsorship dollars go directly toward what keeps an FTC team running: robot parts, competition registration, travel to events, and the tools our students use to design and build. As a small twelve-student team in only our second season, every contribution has an outsized impact on what we're able to build and compete with.

In return, we're glad to feature your name or logo on our website and team materials, shout you out at competitions, and keep you posted on how the season goes. We're still shaping our full sponsorship packet — email us and we'll walk you through the options and current needs.
