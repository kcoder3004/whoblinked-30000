Get Involved

Teach us something
we don't know yet.

We're a twelve-student team still early in our journey — which means a little expert guidance goes a long way. If you've got skills in engineering, coding, design, or business, we'd love to learn from you.

Mentoring Who Blinked can look like whatever fits your schedule — a one-time workshop, a recurring video call, or dropping by our workspace at Princeton STEM Academy to see what we're building. We're especially eager to connect with people working in mechanical engineering, software, CAD, and business/fundraising, but honestly — if you've got something to teach, we want to hear it.

As a young team coming off our rookie season, mentors help us close the gap between "we figured it out ourselves" and "we did it right." That expertise shapes not just our robot, but how the whole team grows.

What Mentoring Looks Like

Occasional video calls or Q&A sessions
In-person visits to our makerspace
One-off workshops on a specific skill
Ongoing season-long guidance
