Who We Are

One season down.
Many more to go.

Who Blinked is a student-led FIRST Tech Challenge team of 12, based at Princeton STEM Academy in Plainsboro, NJ. We competed as rookies in 2025 — now we're back for season two, building on everything that first year taught us.

FTC 30000 Who Blinked got its start in 2025 as a rookie team out of Princeton STEM Academy in Plainsboro, New Jersey. Twelve students came together to design, build, and program a competition robot for the first time — and came out the other side with a lot more experience than we started with.

Our name is a nod to focus under pressure: on the field, on the clock, or staring down a hard engineering problem, we're the team that doesn't look away first. Now in our second season, we're channeling everything we learned as rookies into a sharper robot, a stronger team, and a bigger presence in our community.

We're a small team by design — twelve students who each get real ownership over the build, the code, and the outreach that makes an FTC program run. As we grow, we're looking for mentors, sponsors, and new members who want to grow with us.
