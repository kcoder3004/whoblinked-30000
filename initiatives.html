Beyond the Robot

What we're building this season.

A robot is only part of an FTC team. Here's what Who Blinked is starting to build in our second season — still early days, but this is the direction we're headed.

Community Outreach

We're starting to bring hands-on STEM demos to elementary and middle schools around Plainsboro, sharing what we love about robotics with the next generation.

Open Build Nights

As we grow, we're opening our workspace at Princeton STEM Academy to curious students who want to see an FTC robot come together up close.

Rookie-to-Season-Two Notes

We're documenting our own jump from our rookie year to season two — the lessons and the fixes — to help other new FTC teams get started.

These initiatives are just getting off the ground. If you'd like to help us build any of them out — or have an idea for one we haven't thought of — we'd love to hear from you.
