The Bigger Picture

What is FIRST?

FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is a global robotics community that inspires young people to pursue STEM through hands-on competition. It was founded by inventor Dean Kamen in 1989 and now reaches students from preschool through high school.

The Program Ladder

Five programs, one pipeline.

FIRST offers a program for nearly every age group, each building toward the next.

Ages 4–6

FLL Discover

A playful introductory STEM program designed to spark curiosity about robotics in the youngest learners.

Ages 6–10

FLL Explore

Hands-on LEGO STEM challenges that introduce young students to teamwork and problem-solving.

Ages 9–16

FLL Challenge

Teams build and program LEGO robots to complete real-world missions on a themed playing field.

Ages 12–18

FIRST Tech Challenge — that's us

Students design, build, and program metal-frame robots to compete in alliance-based challenges using Java or Blocks. This is where Who Blinked competes.

High School

FIRST Robotics Competition

High school teams construct industrial-scale robots and compete in fast-paced alliance matches.

Core Values

Gracious Professionalism.

FIRST emphasizes teamwork, innovation, and leadership through what it calls "Gracious Professionalism" — competing hard while treating opponents, mentors, and volunteers with respect. It's a value we try to carry into every match and every build night.

Why FTC?

FIRST Tech Challenge gives high schoolers a real engineering project with a hard deadline — design, CAD, machining, programming, and business, all under one roof. It's the reason Who Blinked exists.
